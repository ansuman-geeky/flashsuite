Frequently Asked Questions About Converting PDFs to Word

How does FlashSuite convert PDF to Word without server uploads?

FlashSuite parses character vectors and transformation matrices using local rendering libraries. It automatically groups text sequences by coordinate thresholds into flowable paragraphs or literal lines, compiling an editable Office Word document directly inside your browser's memory.

What is the difference between 'Flowable Paragraphs' and 'Preserve Lines' layout options?

'Flowable Paragraphs' mathematically calculates horizontal and vertical line proximity, compiling them into smooth, multi-line edit blocks. 'Preserve Lines' keeps literal line breaks exactly as structured, ideal for tabular formats or code snippets.

Is it safe to convert bank statements or legal agreements on FlashSuite?
