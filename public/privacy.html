Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 30, 2026

At FlashSuite, we value the trust you place in our tools. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our custom URL shortening services, dynamic QR code generator, PDF signing and editing tools, and administrative dashboard. Our guiding principle is data minimization: we only collect what is strictly necessary to provide a high-performance, secure experience.

Note on PDF Files: All PDF document editing, signing, and manipulation tools operate 100% in your browser. Your files are processed locally in RAM and are never transmitted to our servers.

1. Information We Collect

A. For Public Users (Link Visitors)

When a user clicks a FlashSuite link, we collect limited technical data to provide analytics to link creators. This includes:

  • IP Address: Used to identify approximate geographic location and prevent fraudulent clicks.
  • User Agent: Information about the browser and operating system to optimize redirection performance.
  • Referrer Data: The website or platform from which the visitor arrived (e.g., Twitter, LinkedIn, or Direct).
  • Timestamp: The exact date and time the link was accessed.

B. For Administrative Users

To manage the FlashSuite platform, we collect:

  • Account Credentials: Usernames and hashed passwords (via Bcrypt) to secure your dashboard.
  • Session Data: We use secure cookies to maintain your login session.

2. 100% Client-Side PDF Processing

Unlike traditional online PDF editors, FlashSuite prioritizes absolute user privacy and data security. All operations on PDF files—including merging, splitting, drawing, signing, and form editing—are executed **entirely in your web browser** using local JavaScript libraries (PDF.js and PDF-lib).

  • No Backend Uploads: Selected PDF documents are read locally as an ArrayBuffer. Your file data is never sent to our backend servers.
  • Zero Storage: Because your files are not uploaded, they are never saved or indexed on our systems. Closing the tab immediately purges the document from your local memory.
  • E2E Privacy: Your sensitive documents remain completely private to you. We cannot view, modify, or leak your PDF content.

3. Google AdSense & Third-Party Cookies

We display Google AdSense advertisements on FlashSuite to fund our free services. To comply with Google's policies, please note the following:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites on the Internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visits to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Opt-Out Options: You can easily opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

4. How We Use Your Data

FlashSuite uses the collected data strictly for the following purposes:

  • Redirection: To accurately send visitors from a short code to the destination URL.
  • Analytics: To generate visual reports (via Chart.js) for link owners regarding link performance.
  • Security: To detect and prevent malicious activity, such as SQL injection attempts or bot-driven click inflation.
  • System Optimization: To monitor server load and ensure "Flash-speed" performance.

5. Data Retention & Security

  • Storage: All link shortener and analytics data is stored in a secure SQLite database environment.
  • Encryption: Administrative passwords are protected using industry-standard salted hashing.
  • Retention: We retain analytics data only as long as necessary to provide historical reporting. Users may delete specific links and their associated analytics at any time via the Admin Dashboard.

6. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Data is only shared when legally mandated by law enforcement or to protect the safety and integrity of our platform.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (such as under GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Request the deletion of your administrative account.
  • Opt-out of certain data collection practices.
