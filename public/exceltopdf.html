How to Convert Excel to PDF Online Free

Converting structured worksheets into high-quality PDF files should be fast, accurate, and secure. FlashSuite's local spreadsheet converter processes Excel files (.xlsx, .xls, .csv, .ods) directly in your browser. This ensures that sensitive business ledgers and client data never upload to remote databases. Follow these quick steps to convert your spreadsheets:

  1. Upload Your Spreadsheet: Select or drag and drop your spreadsheet file (.xlsx, .xls, .csv, .ods) into the drop-zone above.
  2. Select Sheets to Convert: FlashSuite scans your workbook and lists all worksheets. Select or deselect individual sheets using the checkboxes.
  3. Set Layout Options: Choose Landscape or Portrait orientation, pick a table theme (Grid, Striped, Plain), adjust table font size, and set a custom header background color if desired.
  4. Convert & Download: Click **Convert to PDF** to format the table. Once ready, click **Download PDF** to save the file locally.

Why Choose FlashSuite's Client-Side Excel Converter?

Frequently Asked Questions About Excel to PDF Conversion

Why does my generated PDF look cut off or wrap weirdly?

Excel spreadsheets often contain very wide tables. To ensure your table fits onto the page, try switching the page orientation to **Landscape** and setting the font size scaling to **Small (7pt)**.

How does the 'Bank Statement Converter' ensure security?

When converting sensitive financial statements or ledger sheets, FlashSuite processes all calculations and table formatting locally within your browser. No database, server, or cloud service intercepts your file content.

Can I convert multi-sheet Excel workbooks?

Yes! When you upload an Excel workbook, FlashSuite automatically detects all worksheets and displays a checklist. You can choose to convert only specific sheets or compile the entire workbook.

What spreadsheet formats are supported?

We support Microsoft Excel (.xlsx, .xls), comma-separated values (.csv), OpenDocument spreadsheets (.ods), and tab-separated text files.
