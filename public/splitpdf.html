How to Split PDF Pages Online Free

Extracting specific pages or separating a large PDF into individual documents is quick and effortless with FlashSuite. Everything happens in-memory inside your local web browser, meaning your records are never exposed to remote internet hosts. Here is how to split your PDFs:

  1. Upload Your PDF Document: Click inside the upload zone above or drag your PDF directly onto it. FlashSuite will instantly parse the document locally and identify the total page count.
  2. Select Extraction Method: Choose between two high-performance modes:
    • Extract Selected Pages: Enter the specific page indices or ranges you need (e.g., `1-3, 5, 8-10`). FlashSuite will combine only these pages into a single new document.
    • Split All Pages: Instantly separate every single page of the uploaded PDF into standalone, individual files packed in a ZIP folder.
  3. Download Your Pages: Click the primary Split PDF button. Within a fraction of a second, click the green Download Output button to save the new PDF or ZIP folder directly to your machine.

Frequently Asked Questions About Splitting PDFs

How does FlashSuite split PDF pages without server uploads?

FlashSuite uses advanced client-side JavaScript libraries (pdf-lib) to read the document's page indices directly inside your browser's RAM, extracting the selected streams and saving a new file locally.

Can I extract specific page ranges like 1-3 and page 5 together?

Yes! Our custom range compiler parses inputs like '1-3, 5' to combine pages 1, 2, 3, and 5 into a single output PDF document in that exact sequence.

What happens when I choose 'Split All Pages'?

Every page is compiled into its own standalone PDF file in-memory, and all pages are packaged into a single downloadable ZIP file instantly.

Is there a file size limit for extracting pages?

No, there are no artificial file limits. FlashSuite is 100% free and processes documents locally, utilizing your local system's capabilities.
