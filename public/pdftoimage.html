Frequently Asked Questions About Converting PDFs to Images

How does FlashSuite convert PDF pages to images without server uploads?

FlashSuite utilizes the official Mozilla PDF.js rendering engine locally in your web browser. It parses the document's vector streams, draws them onto high-definition HTML5 canvas components at a premium 2.0x zoom factor, and exports them directly to PNG/JPG blobs in your device's memory.

What is the difference between PNG and JPG format options?

PNG is a lossless image format that retains maximum visual clarity, transparent layers, and sharp font glyphs. JPG uses lossy compression to significantly reduce storage sizes, making it ideal for scanned photographs or large multi-page sheets.

Can I download all the extracted page images at once?

Yes! Our compiler bundles every rendered image page into a single compressed ZIP archive locally in-memory, letting you download all high-resolution files with a single click.

Is there a page extraction limit on FlashSuite?

No! FlashSuite is entirely free, does not require registrations or emails, and places no page counts or file size restrictions on your conversions.
